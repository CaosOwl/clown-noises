Clown Noise Board

Tap a tile. Honk responsibly.

Swap mode: tap any tile to load your own sound onto it: an audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV) or a short video such as a screen recording. Silence at the start and end is cut off. Tap Swap sounds again when you're done.

On iPhone, flip the silent switch off if you hear nothing. Sounds you load with Swap sounds stay on this device only; to put a sound on every device, add it to the sounds/ folder of the repo.

Clown horn ("free sound eff") and circus music ("Gaming Sound FX") supplied by the owner. Other recordings: Oculus Audio Pack 1 by Meta, licensed CC BY 4.0 (laughs, boing, banjo, fail, drum roll, snare crash, ta-da); sfx-kit by Wyzowl (applause, fireworks, popper, pop), rubberduck and Thimras on OpenGameArt (spring, gong, cannon) and rubberduckie on Freesound (cymbal swell), all CC0; ship's bell by Nigel K, public domain. Fallback organ version of "Entry of the Gladiators" (Julius Fučík, 1897): note data from the Fairy Lights mod by Paul Fulham (MIT).
